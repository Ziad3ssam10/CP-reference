#### Heron 
$$s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}$$
 $$Area = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$$
 $a, b, c$ there are the lengths of the sides

### Law of sines and cosines 
$$\frac{a}{sin(A)} = \frac{b}{sin(B)} = \frac{c}{sin(C)} = d$$

$$c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos(C)$$
$a, b, c$: lengths, $A, B, C$: opposite angles, $d$: circumcircle
